About Campus Ride

Campus Ride is a smart campus transportation management system built to make daily travel easier, safer, and more transparent for students, drivers, and administrators. The platform delivers real-time bus tracking, organized route schedules, and role-specific dashboards — so everyone has the right information at the right time. Administrators efficiently manage buses and student allocations; drivers update live location details; students instantly check their assigned bus route and pickup timing. Campus Ride reduces waiting time and brings clarity to campus commuting.

Key Features

📍
Live Bus Tracking

Drivers update real-time location data, allowing students and admins to see exactly where each bus is at any moment.

📊
Admin Dashboard

Admins get a powerful control panel to manage bus allocations, student records, and driver assignments.

🔔
Pickup Notifications

Students receive timely alerts about their bus arrival status, eliminating unnecessary waiting at stops.
